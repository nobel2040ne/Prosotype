The five channels

Each has one owner and one input. They do not share state, and three pairs are explicitly independent.

01Colour turn

05Hold and lift

A word the speaker held rises off the line and lands as it turns. What it detects is isolation, not sustain: the silence either side, measured over inter-onset intervals. Size and lift are mutually exclusive — a word that swells does not leave the line.

—The character wave, in syllables

Not a sixth channel: texture under the colour turn, and it moves a syllable at a time — read off the film, seen lifts as se + en, button as but + ton. Inside a group each letter sits at a slightly different angle, which is what makes it read as hand-set rather than as a block.

Amplitude still scales with how far each letter departs from its own word's size, so a loud word holds its letters together while an ordinary-volume word lets the lift carry them. The colour keeps travelling one letter at a time underneath — two clocks, not one.

The playhead

What makes this a CWI renderer rather than a subtitle track: 2.2.1 needs the line on screen before it is spoken, and a live recogniser cannot produce text early.

Captions present from a clock behind the acoustic one

1.75 s behind. The delay is the lag, one for one — nothing is predicted.

Colouring only up to now − delay leaves real, recognised, still-uncoloured text ahead of the colour.

Read-ahead is a per-word floor, not a time delay

420 ms minimum lead before a word may turn.

The recogniser releases words in bursts, so a delay moves the mean lead and leaves its spread alone: at 1.75 s, 42% of words still turned within 100 ms of appearing. The floor takes that to 0%.

Do not gate the turn on speaker attribution

Text arrives at a median 0.62 s; durable attribution at 4.48 s.

The turn happens on time in whatever colour is known, or it does not happen on time at all.

Word events are not a clock source

They are replayed to every new connection, and an old timestamp reads as a restart.

Words under an older epoch must settle, never be re-derived — re-deriving puts them in the future and turns a whole stage of read captions white again.

Rules that aren't visible in the table

The word grows from its baseline. It does not move.

No translateY, no box-bottom pivot.

Wrong four times.

Everything is frozen at first sight

Duration, axes, sweep, hold gap and turn moment are computed once per word and survive remounts.

Recomputing any of them under a running animation is the bug this project re-commits most.

Size and lift are mutually exclusive, and both gates are binary

A word that swells does not leave the line; a word that lifts shows no crest and no weight.

Every word carries the 2.2.3 pop, so a proportional gate taxes a word for a cue it never asked for.

Two clocks, never collapsed

--motion-duration rides the speech rate; --crest-duration rides emphasis.

Collapsing them made emphatic words 4.7× too fast in one direction and ordinary words 4.8× too slow in the other.

Never make the reveal a typewriter

Progressive appearance destroys the read-ahead 2.2.1 exists for.

The line must be there to read before it is spoken.

Reduced motion keeps the colour turn

It drops only the geometry — pin the phase rather than cancelling the animation.

Cancelling the word's animation also cancels the turn, which is the one cue that carries meaning.
