What it is

What a caption carries beyond the words.

A subtitle keeps the words and discards the rest. Caption with Intention, from the Chicago Hearing Society, restores three: speaker as colour, timing as motion, delivery as typography — derived live, from a microphone, without transmitting audio.

The pipeline

From capture to glyph, entirely on the local machine.

Language is chosen before capture and locked. Everything runs on the local CPU; the only network is a one-time model download and the LAN link to the node.

ConstraintReason
Gain is applied to the recognizer's copy only Prosody measures loudness from the captured signal; gaining it first maps a whisper and a shout to the same type size.
Word onsets are never synthesised Prosody, the motion clock and the read-ahead floor all key off per-word start and end times, so a recognizer without them can supply text at the verifier boundary and nowhere else.
Utterances are reconciled, not rebuilt Streaming and verified transcripts mismatch on nearly every utterance. Rebuilding the block would flash the sentence at every pause, so matches are corrected in place and insertions added at their spoken position.

Internals

One process decides, one stream carries, one reducer draws.

Python decides and the browser draws. What crosses between them is a stream of word events, and the renderer is a pure function of it.

The playhead

What makes this a CWI renderer rather than a subtitle track.

CWI needs the line on screen before it is spoken, and a live recogniser cannot produce text early. So captions present from a clock running behind the acoustic one. Nothing is predicted.

RuleFigureWhy it is not the obvious thing
The playhead trails the acoustic clock 1.75 s Colouring only up to the present minus the delay leaves recognised text ahead of the colour.
Read-ahead is a per-word floor 420 ms Words arrive in bursts, so a delay moves the mean lead without narrowing its spread. Only a per-word floor guarantees the margin.
The turn is never gated on the speaker — Text arrives long before a durable speaker decision, so the turn happens on time in whatever colour is known.

The studio

Two design systems, and the boundary is the word.

The chrome follows Apple’s design language; the captions follow CWI, which outranks it. The line is literal: anything inside a caption word is CWI, everything else is Apple.

Speaker colour is the only colour that carries information, so everything around it is achromatic — and a sound label sits inside the dial, because a non-speech event has a direction too.

What it can't do

Known limitations.

Which word grows Emphasis is derived from the acoustics, and the reference film’s own choices correlate −0.018 with ours. Shape, timing and amplitude match; which word is a transcriber’s judgement and no rule recovers it.
First colour Text lands at a median 0.62 s and durable attribution at 4.48 s, so a word can turn before its speaker is known. It turns grey rather than guessing.
Korean expression Word motion measures the same as English, but the available Korean face has no width axis, so §2.3.10 is silently dropped.
The motors The mapping and wire protocol are tested offline. They have never run against real hardware.
